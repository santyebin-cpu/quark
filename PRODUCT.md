## Platform

web

## Users

The public marketing website of Qark Energy. Four confirmed audiences, all served by the same pages:

- **Utilities and industry:** power buyers, industrial parks, data centres and desalination operators evaluating Qark One as a source of electricity or steam.
- **Government and partners:** India's nuclear establishment (DAE, AERB, NPCIL), state governments and engineering partners assessing the design.
- **Investors:** deciding whether to fund Qark Energy.
- **Engineers:** reactor physics, thermal-hydraulics, mechanical and licensing talent considering joining.

## Product Purpose

Qark Energy is developing **Qark One**, a thorium-fuelled small modular reactor for India. The website explains what Qark One is, why India needs it, and how it works, so each audience can take its next step: talk to the team, partner, invest or apply.

## Positioning

**More power for India.** The user's lead message; the framing is delegated. Qark One turns thorium, a fuel India holds in abundance, into firm, carbon-free power through a design that runs on natural circulation and passive safety, carrying forward the thorium goal of India's three-stage nuclear programme.

## Operating Context

- Visitors evaluate an early-stage company: the reference design is at **concept** status (drawing QE-GA-001, rev A, 2026-10-03).
- National context the content may cite: India's target of 100 GW of nuclear capacity by 2047 and its three-stage nuclear power programme, which ends in thorium.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Reference design data, from the user's general-arrangement drawing (`src/assets/plant/blueprint.webp`):

- Reference: BARC AHWR-300 LEU (published data; dimensions approximate; not for construction)
- 920 MWth / approx. 300 MWe
- Coolant: boiling light water, natural circulation, no primary pumps
- Moderator: heavy water (D2O), low pressure and temperature
- Fuel: (Th-HALEU)O2, 54-pin clusters; 452 vertical fuel channels at 225 mm square pitch; 3.5 m active core
- Main heat transport: 7 MPa, 285 °C coolant outlet; four steam drums; approx. 39 m loop height for natural circulation
- On-power, top-entry refuelling
- Two independent shutdown systems: SDS-1 shut-off rods, SDS-2 liquid poison injection
- 72-hour passive grace period from an approx. 8,000 m³ gravity-driven water pool; isolation condensers remove decay heat passively

Undecided or not yet public, and must not be invented: the first plant site, partners or MoUs, team names and bios, licensing status, timelines, costs and funding. An office address exists but has not been supplied yet.

The four photographic renders are illustrative concept art. Their equipment (for example the vessel with helical steam generators) does not match the pressure-tube reference design, so never label parts on them with reference-design terms.

## Product Principles

1. **Lead with abundance.** Every page answers how Qark One brings more firm power to India.
2. **True at concept stage.** State design intent and published reference data; never imply approvals, sites, partners or dates that do not exist.
3. **India's own fuel and engineering.** Thorium, the three-stage programme and Indian manufacturing are the through-line, not borrowed Western narratives.
4. **Substance for experts.** Principal data stays visible and precise, because regulators, utilities and engineers will check it.
